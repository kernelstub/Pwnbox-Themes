# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be respectful. No harassment, no bigotry, no spam. Credit the people whose
palettes, wallpapers or code you build on. Do not submit artwork you do not
have the right to share.

## The long version

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. The full text applies as written; the summary above is not a
substitute for it.

## Theme-specific expectations

- Do not upload wallpapers, screenshots or palettes that you do not have
  permission to redistribute.
- Remove personal information from screenshots before committing them.
- Do not use a theme to distribute malware, scripts, trackers or hidden
  content. Themes are data: `theme.conf` plus images.
- Do not impersonate another author or present a port as official without
  permission.
- Give reviewers time. This is a community project, not a paid service.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to [kern@skuntir.com](mailto:kern@skuntir.com).
Reports are treated confidentially. Repeated or severe violations result in
removal from the project (issue locks, PR rejection, blocking) at the
maintainer's discretion. Content that violates a licence or contains private
data will be removed.
